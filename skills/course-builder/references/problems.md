# Course risk checklist

Go through the whole list, but name to the user only the risks that are real for this course, each with a concrete fix. Nobody reads a "just in case" list of risks.

## Meaning and audience
- **Vague learner.** "For anyone interested in X" is a course for no one. Fix: narrow to one profile from question 3.
- **No concrete question / promise.** You can't check whether the course worked. Fix: phrase "after the course the learner can …" measurably.
- **The course answers the client's question, not the learner's.** The client wants to tell everything they know. Fix: check every lesson for "does this move them closer to the answer".
- **Level mismatch.** Jargon for beginners, basics for experts. Fix: entry test or two tracks.

## Volume and structure
- **Content doesn't fit the duration** (or it's padded). Fix: recount minutes per lesson, cut ballast.
- **Slow start.** Lesson one is history and theory, no result. Fix: a quick practical win in lesson 1.
- **No practice / assessment.** Knowledge without application is forgotten. Fix: an exercise in every lesson, a final project.
- **Lessons too long.** Videos over 10–15 minutes get finished less often. Fix: split them.

## Content
- **Going out of date.** Laws, prices, software UIs change. Fix: move changeable parts into separate updatable blocks, state the date of validity.
- **Disputed claims stated as facts.** Fix: from the research summary, present disputed things as disputed.
- **YMYL (health, finance, law, psychology).** Risk of harm and complaints. Fix: disclaimers, specialist review, no personal recommendations.
- **Contradicting the client's materials.** Fix: discuss with the client before production, not silently.

## Rights and legal
- **Images, music, fonts without a license.** Fix: own / licensed stock / generated with commercial rights.
- **Voice and face.** Voice cloning or an avatar only with consent. Fix: written consent.
- **Third-party material in the course** (quotes, diagrams from books). Fix: short quotes with a reference, your own diagrams.
- **Tool licenses** (e.g. unslop is CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution). Fix: keep the attribution.
- **Promising outcomes** ("you'll earn $10,000"). Risk of complaints and advertising-law violations. Fix: promise a skill, not income.

## Production
- **Video costs more than it looks.** Fix: pilot one lesson, then decide on scale; move some lessons to PDF.
- **Environment not ready** (no Node/Remotion/ffmpeg, no voice connector). Fix: check before promising.
- **No consistent style** across lessons. Fix: design direction via unslop on the pilot, then a template.
- **"AI look" in visuals** (gradients, glows, emoji). Fix: audit against `skills/unslop/references/signs.md`.
- **Synthetic voice mispronounces stresses and terms.** Fix: pronunciation dictionary, listen to the pilot.
- **Edits after rendering.** Fix: approve the script and storyboard before rendering.

## Delivery
- **The platform doesn't accept the format** (video size, SCORM, duration). Fix: confirm the platform early.
- **Accessibility.** No subtitles, poor contrast. Fix: subtitles always, contrast check.
- **Mobile learners.** Fix: readable fonts in videos, no tiny text in PDFs.
